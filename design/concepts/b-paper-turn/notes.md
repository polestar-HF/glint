# Glint · b-paper-turn

**Concept.** Translation as turning the page. The tile is paper carrying a capture frame, and its fourth corner curls back to reveal the ink-blue translated page beneath.

**Gradient.** Colour appears as light on the curl's pearl underside: cyan at one end, peach at the other, white across the bend. Ink blue is a page, not a glow.

**Texture.** Two-scale paper grain (fine fibre plus faint mottle) on both pages.

**Material.** Warm off-white stock lit from the top left. A crease shadow at the fold, a soft shadow on the blue page, and ambient occlusion where the curl lifts.

**Small sizes.** A 45° fold anti-aliases cleanly. The curl becomes a flat triangle, with two lines at 16/24. Risks: the light tile needs its 1 px outline on a light taskbar, and at 16 it nears a generic document icon. The blue corner carries recognition.
