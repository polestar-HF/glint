# Glass pane

The capture frame becomes a frosted-glass slab on a page of text lines that run past it.

- **Gradient**: the same top-lit graphite tile. The glass is white, 13 % → 5 %.
- **Texture**: about 4 % grain. The frost is the page blurred and shifted 8 px down (refraction), so the lines bend at the glass edge.
- **Material**: a top inner highlight, a soft cast shadow, and cyan/peach dispersion where light enters and leaves.
- **Brackets**: light caught on the slab's four corners, brightest at the upper left.
- **Light**: only the middle line's "recognized" stretch turns ink blue → cyan, glowing through the frost.
- **Small sizes**: the glass becomes a lighter flat square and the corners become bracket pixels, with one gradient.
- **Risks**: busier than Satin at 48 px. The corners are 1 px at 16 px, and below 32 px the frost reads as blur.
