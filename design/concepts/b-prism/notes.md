# Glint · b-prism

**Concept.** The name, taken literally. A faceted crystal sits on the text line inside the capture frame: matte source text goes in, ink-blue light comes out. A hairline glint catches the apex.

**Gradient.** Colour appears only as light. Cyan and peach dispersion fan through the glass along the line, and the exit line glows ink blue. The facets stay neutral; one carries the ink body colour.

**Texture.** Fine soft-light grain on the graphite tile; the glass stays smooth.

**Material.** Facet values, white ridges and a contact shadow make the glass, with the line seen refracted through the lower facets. The brackets are satin, and the tile has a rim light.

**Small sizes.** 16/24/32 drop glint, grain and glow. Risks: at 16 the crystal sits 1 px from the brackets, and a thicker glint would read as an AI sparkle.
