## Changes

### UI & Visual Design
- **Prism Tactile UI redesign.** Both the translation popup and Settings window adopt tactile layered cards on Windows 11 Mica: pill language selectors, a softly recessed editable source container, an elevated primary translation card with a Prism Cyan (`#0E8CB8`) accent, status dot, and primary badge, pill re-translate CTA when editing, and soft error cards.
- **Refreshed app icon & brand lockup.** Dark charcoal squircle with floating white capture brackets and a 4-facet luminous cyan crystal, integrated into the Settings sidebar and About hero alongside the version pill.

### Performance & Reliability
- **Faster overlay startup & multi-monitor capture.** Reuses warm Direct2D/Win32 overlay surfaces (releasing automatically after 5 minutes idle), captures monitors and window snap targets in parallel, and syncs first paint via `DwmFlush`.
- **Faster OCR cold/warm runs.** Persists ONNX Runtime optimized models on disk, caps intra-op threads to 6 to reduce contention on high-thread CPUs, vectorizes CTC argmax, and extends default OCR idle unload to 15 minutes (`900s`).
- **Improved translation reliability & networking.** Enables HTTP/2 and automatic system proxy detection in the shared HTTP client, and uses a standard browser User-Agent on the keyless Google endpoint to reduce rate-limit failures.
